# Pressure pad

This model is an **original CanFactory design**. It is not derived from any third-party
model: `generator.scad` was written directly in OpenSCAD for this application, so there
is no `reference/` folder with an original STL or source to preserve.

## Parts it is sized for

The nut chamber and head pocket are sized from the parts library's published dimensions of
ISO 10511 and ISO 4032 nuts and ISO 4017 hexagon head screws (largest values). The
standards themselves are not reproduced.
