# Wand holder

This model is an **original CanFactory design**. It is not derived from any third-party
model: `generator.scad` was written directly in OpenSCAD for this application, so there
is no `reference/` folder with an original STL or source to preserve.

## What it is sized for

The clip grips scratching posts 90 to 110 mm across their sisal rope, from TRIXIE's ø 9 cm
replacement trunks (article 43991, the parts library's `trixie-43991-sisal-trunk`) to its
ø 11 cm ones. Only the listed sizes are used. The socket is sized from the wand handle's
diameter you enter.
