# Adjustable miter box

This model is an **original CanFactory design**. It is not derived from any third-party
model: `generator.scad` was written directly in OpenSCAD for this application, so there
is no `reference/` folder with an original STL or source to preserve. Its knobs and thrust
pads are rendered by the pressure pad's generator (`models/pressure-pad/generator.scad`),
also an original CanFactory design.

## Parts it is sized for

Nut pockets, channels, sockets and holes are sized from the parts library's published
dimensions of ISO 4032 and ISO 10511 nuts, ISO 4017 screws, stainless ISO 2338 (A1) dowel
pins and DIN 7997 wood screws (largest values). The standards themselves are not reproduced.
The scale's labels use Liberation Sans Bold (`models/fonts`, SIL Open Font License).
