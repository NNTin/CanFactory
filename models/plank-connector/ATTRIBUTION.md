# Plank connector

This model is an **original CanFactory design**. It is not derived from any third-party
model: `generator.scad` was written directly in OpenSCAD for this application, so there
is no `reference/` folder with an original STL or source to preserve.

## Standards

The screw-hole diameters are the clearance holes of **DIN EN 20273** (identical to
ISO 273, *Fasteners — Clearance holes for bolts and screws*), in its fine (H12), medium
(H13) and coarse (H14) series. Only the published hole diameters are used; the standard
itself is not reproduced.
