# Outside-corner toggle latch

Derivative of CanFactory’s [toggle latch](../toggle-latch/ATTRIBUTION.md), reconstructed from “Toggle Latch” on
[MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/de/models/625647-toggle-latch), itself a remix of Hacky97’s
[“M3 toggle corner latch”](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5993215).

Original creator: Hacky97. Original license: CC BY 4.0. MakerWorld remix and this derivative:
[CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Credit the creators, indicate changes, and do not use
commercially. This attribution grants no additional rights to the originals.

Changes for CanFactory: lengthened the base’s mounting plate; added a perpendicular, gusseted mounting flange to the
catch; moved both pairs of countersunk wood-screw holes beyond a plank butt joint; added plank-thickness and screw-length
validation. The lever and link are reused unchanged from `../toggle-latch/reference/`, including the loose-pivot option.
The hook and knuckle profiles are preserved verbatim. Original reference files remain in that directory.

The digital geometry has been checked, but this variant has not been physically fit- or load-tested.
