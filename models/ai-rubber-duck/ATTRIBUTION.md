# AI rubber duck

The duck bodies, heads, mechanical joints, and assembly design are original
CanFactory work based on the [approved concept images](../../docs/concepts/ai-rubber-ducks/README.md).
The model geometry is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The OpenAI knot outline comes from
[Simple Icons 13.0.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/13.0.0/icons/openai.svg).
Its original SVG is preserved in `reference/openai.svg`. Simple Icons publishes
its contribution under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/13.0.0/LICENSE.md).
The SVG was converted with this repository's `svgToLogo` and `decodeLogo` helpers;
the resulting polygon rings are embedded in the self-contained SCAD generator.

Claude's starburst, the terminal-inspired Codex face, and the Anthropic-inspired
AI monogram are geometric interpretations of the concepts. Brand names and
marks belong to their respective owners; the model license does not grant
trademark rights or imply endorsement.
