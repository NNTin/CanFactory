# Removable cat-port and tunnel-access panels

New modular catio designs use **removable panels with turn buttons**. The **Cat-port closure** control on the
window insert page owns the window panel; **Enclosure cat-port closure** on the whole-catio page owns the enclosure
panels. Either can still use the original vertical sliding gate. The detailed tunnel page reads both owners' choices.

**Roofs are unchanged.** This feature changes no roof material, default, geometry, support, fixing or roof cut-list entry.
The original design sheets remain historical studies, including their sliding gates; procedural geometry and current
settings take precedence.

## Reference and proposed mechanism

[PawHut's tunnel](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0GLFJRNKD/) shows
[removable mesh panels retained by screw-pivoted tabs](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/81IrzxOvi-L._AC_SL1500_.jpg).
Rotate the tabs clear, then remove the entire panel. These are not hinged doors or fold-down ramps. The photos do not
establish the concealed seat, tolerances, locking details or strength.

Our proposed geometry is shared by every preview:

- A continuous backstop supports the panel against inward pressure; four turn buttons retain it against outward pressure.
- A locating surround leaves 2 mm clearance at every edge. The free-overlap window/access panels overlap the clear port by 10 mm per side. Enclosure panels instead seat on the existing floor at their lower edge; the floor and three-sided surround form their seat.
- Each tab has a captive locking indexing pin. Retract the pins 12 mm, rotate the tabs 90°, then withdraw the panel
  **normal to its opening**, not upwards in tracks. Reverse that sequence to close and positively lock it.
- The window panel is 12 mm exterior plywood with a continuous compressed gasket, for draught closure. Outdoor panels
  have 30 × 12 mm timber rims and mesh with its cut edges continuously clamped under flush-inset 12 × 4 mm battens.
- Stops, surrounds, tabs, custom pivots/indexing pins and fixings are in the parts lists. Available standard wood screws
  and pivot washers link to the parts library. Custom pins/pivots are schematic hardware, not new printable models.

These dimensions are our prototype design, **not measured PawHut specifications**. The drawing is not a structural
certification and does not prove cat-proof retention. Select and physically verify the locking hardware before fabrication.

## Window fit and operation

The removable panel needs no full-height travel space above the port. Only the sliding option keeps that validation.
The removable option checks its surround, closed-sash clearance and withdrawal through the **open** window, including clearance over the fixed sill lip. The default low wedge-mounted threshold fails that withdrawal check: choose a sliding gate or an attachment with a higher threshold. The insert
continues to carry no tunnel load, and its screen hooks, spreader feet and docking hardware retain their existing roles.

The lower locating surround requires a local notch at the room-side threshold/sill edge: 20 mm into the insert, down to
28 mm below the port floor, across the surround's width. The outdoor bearing timber and threshold fasteners remain intact.
The preview and cut list include this notch; its effect on the timber must be checked physically.

The window-insert, tunnel and insert–tunnel coupling pages show the same selected closure. Their **Doors & access panels**
checkboxes show seated/retained versus removed states, persisted with camera and assembly settings. The window page requires
an open sash before removal; the coupling page closes/disables the window panel when the docking latches are released.
Enclosure panels seat on the existing solid threshold; it completes the seat beneath their three-sided surround, so the walking surface remains continuous. The whole-catio schematic uses that floor-seated arrangement too. All closures remain shut until assembly is complete. The enclosure's human maintenance doors remain hinged.

## Tunnel maintenance access

**Tunnel maintenance access** adds a right-side removable mesh panel to each sufficiently long straight section, including
sloped sections. Short sections and angle collars keep fixed mesh. For mitred ends, the opening is a rectangle within the
common usable side span, not a distorted cut following the mitre.

The opening is at most 300 mm wide, leaves at least 80 mm at each end of the common side span, and runs from 50 mm above
the floor to 50 mm below the clear ceiling. Full-height side jambs fix into existing rails and carry the surround. The
existing net hood/welded side stock cut is retained; cut only the side aperture and clamp its edges under the fixed surround.
No mesh remains across the aperture behind the removable panel. The roof is not cut or reconfigured.

The whole-catio preview retains its original illustrative 20 cm port/floor levels; the detailed insert/tunnel pages use their measured-profile-derived threshold instead. Do not fabricate from the schematic whole-catio window alone.

The whole-catio preview offers equivalent side access on its schematic 50 cm sections. Its switch and dimensions belong
to that schematic configuration; the detailed tunnel's independent route/settings remain owned by the tunnel page, as before.

## Compatibility and physical checks

Saved configs without the new fields retain sliding gates and fixed tunnel side mesh, their dimensions and viewing state.
Fresh designs and explicit resets select removable panels. Existing roof settings and behavior are untouched. A reset changes only the settings owned by that page; reset the window insert separately to change its closure.

Before removing **any maintenance panel**, secure cats elsewhere. Before disconnecting a tunnel, close and positively
retain both end gates. Leave unused ports shut. Store removed panels away from the route, not as ramps or loose obstacles.

Before use, physically test bidirectional panel push-out and tilt/flex resistance, accidental pin/tab release, pivot and
frame strength, gasket compression, sash/withdrawal clearance, and interference with couplings and support straps. Verify
wood edge distances, screw-tip containment, covered mesh ends and paw/tail pinch points. This repository's geometry and
browser tests cannot substitute for those tests; no physical validation has been performed for this feature.
